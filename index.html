Our mission

Made for slow living, handmade stories, and creative freedom.

House of Briar is a place for independent designers to share their work without pressure, gatekeeping, or noise. This is a space for one-of-a-kind pieces, upcycled finds, and hand-painted goods that feel personal.

We believe creativity should be soft, honest, and welcoming — no judgment, just beauty and possibility.
